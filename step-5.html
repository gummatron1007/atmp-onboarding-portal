Section Three — Video

Five video formats. Filmed on your phone.

Vertical phone video drives the cheapest leads we generate. The good news: you don't need a videographer, a script, or studio lighting. You need 60 seconds and your phone.

Why this is for you
Every top campaign shares one thing: vertical phone video from the owner.
Homeowners want to see who they're hiring before they enquire — campaigns that skip this take 3× longer to find a winner.

The five videos we make for you

A quick overview of each format and how it differs from the others.

  1. Selfie / Talking head
    You, speaking straight to the camera. The only format where you talk directly to the viewer — it builds the personal connection none of the others can.
  2. Project showcase
    A walkthrough of a finished job, highlighting the craftsmanship. About the work, not you — proof of quality rather than personality.
  3. Client testimonials
    A happy client on camera sharing their experience. The only format where someone else does the talking — third-party trust you can't create yourself.
  4. Transformation
    The "before" state, then the finished "after" from the same angle. No talking needed — the dramatic visual shift does all the selling.
  5. Expert / Educational
    Tips, trade secrets, or advice from a job site. Sells nothing directly — it positions you as the trusted local expert before they ever enquire.
FYI — why the talking head is the #1 format

Homeowners hire people, not businesses. When they see your face, hear your voice, and feel like they already know you — the gap from "ad" to "enquiry" collapses. Our top-performing campaigns are 2–3× cheaper per lead when the owner is on camera versus when they aren't. It's the single highest-leverage 60 seconds you'll spend in this onboarding.

Download an app called Teleprompter on your phone — 2 easy steps

  1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) on your phone
  2. Search "teleprompter" and install a free app (e.g. Teleprompter Premium, BIGVU)

The app allows you to:

  • Paste in the script
  • Display the script on your screen
  • Record yourself while reading the script
  • Look directly at the camera while following the words on screen

This makes the video look natural, as if you're speaking directly to the camera.

How to Record Your Video

Don't worry about making the video perfect. The goal is to record a natural video of you speaking directly to the camera. We'll handle the editing, cuts, captions, and other improvements afterward.

Once you receive the script:

Step 1 of 10
Open the script on Teleprompter
  • Open the app
  • Paste the script into the Teleprompter
  • Adjust the text size so it's easy to read
  • Set the scrolling speed to a comfortable pace
  • Position the script as close as possible to the phone's camera

Quick Checklist Before Sending

Before sending the video, check:

✓Your face is clearly visible
✓The video is recorded vertically
✓The camera is stable
✓Your face is well-lit
✓The background is clean and relevant
✓Your voice is clear
✓There is minimal background noise
✓You followed the script
✓The video file is saved and ready to send
💪
Most importantly: don't overthink it. You don't need to get the perfect take. Just speak naturally and give it a go. The editing team will take care of the rest.
