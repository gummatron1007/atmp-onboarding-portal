Complete Guide to Recording High-Converting Video Ads

To get the best possible results from your video campaigns, we utilise five core video formats. Recording these is straightforward, and you do not need a professional camera crew. Simply follow the framework below to nail your setup and delivery.

The 5 Core Video Formats to Record

  1. Selfie / Talking Head
    Hold your phone at eye level and speak directly to the lens. Keep it casual and authentic to build a personal connection with the viewer.
  2. Project Showcase
    Film a horizontal walkthrough of a finished job site. Walk the viewer through the project, highlighting your craftsmanship and pointing out specific details.
  3. Testimonials
    Hand the phone to a happy client on-site or set it up on a tripod. Have them share their genuine experience working with your team.
  4. Transformations
    Capture the dramatic visual shift. Film the messy or outdated "before" state of a job site, and then film the final, pristine "after" result from the exact same angle.
  5. Educational Videos
    Stand in front of a clean background or an active job site and share helpful tips, trade secrets, or industry advice to position yourself as the trusted local expert.
📐
Formatting note: Always record your videos in a wide, horizontal format. Avoid tight, squished vertical videos so we can maximise the layout and visual impact of your ads.

Why testimonials beat polished reviews

Strangers trust strangers more than they trust you. A 30-second phone clip from a happy client — even shaky and badly lit — outperforms a polished review video. The roughness is the proof. The audience knows polish costs money; raw doesn't. Authenticity sells, polish makes them sceptical.

Our highest-performing format for renovation

The dramatic before/after reveal is the cheapest cost-per-lead format we run for renovation businesses. The visual contrast does all the selling for you — no pitch, no claim, no script. The audience sees what's possible for their own home in 15 seconds. It's why this is our default opener for pool, deck, kitchen, and bathroom campaigns.

The 3-shot structure that wins
1. Before — held still3–5 seconds. Tripod or steady. Worst possible angle of the space.
2. The reveal cutHard cut on the beat drop. No fade. The contrast is the moment.
3. After — same angleIdentical framing as the before. Hold for 5+ seconds. Let it land.
Optional: B-roll5 seconds of detail shots after the main reveal.

Why this format wins the educated buyer

Most homeowners don't know what a good finished job actually looks like. They can't tell a proper weld from a cosmetic one, a real waterproof membrane from paint, a structural fix from a band-aid. When you teach them what to look for, two things happen at once: you become the expert in their head, and every other quote they get starts to look suspicious. You've raised the bar — and only you can clear it.
